Overview
Name: PlayLS (Lazy Susan for Playlists)
Goal: Help users keep their Spotify playlists fresh by rotating songs in and out on a regular schedule.

Problem
Oftentimes, Spotify users will create playlists that are a compilation of their favorite songs at the time. Over time, our song preferences change and we start skipping songs every time they come on. Sometimes songs we don’t really listen to anymore will stay on our playlists for months as we skip them every time and tell ourselves we need to remove it.


This app aims to help users conveniently maintain a fresh playlist by tracking when songs were added to a playlist and every set amount of days, prompting the user to choose to keep or remove the song. Additionally, it will have features to help users find new music or return to old favorites, so that the playlist is constantly updating.

Solution
A rotating playlist that:
Prompts users to review each song after a set time


Lets them choose which songs to keep or remove by swiping left or right


User can replace removed songs with new recommendations or old songs they might want to return to  [PHASE 2]



Core Features
Spotify login with OAuth


Connect or create a Spotify playlist
Will be called a Rotation once connected
[Only playlists the user owns or collaborates on. Spotify is always the source of truth for playlist contents.]


User sets a rotation interval (e.g., every 30 days)
Songs will be added to a queue every time interval starting from the time the song was added (or the time Rotation was created if already in playlist)
[Changed: a song is due at added_at + N even if it was already in the playlist; after a keep it's due at keep_date + N. Notifications are not planned.]


Interface for reviewing songs and removing or keeping them
User can, at their will, go to this queue of songs and start swiping left and right to “rotate” their playlist
[Actions are keep / skip / remove. Skip leaves the song in the playlist and it stays due. Removals are saved per swipe and sent to Spotify in batches when the user leaves.]


Recommend new songs based on user preferences or current playlist  [DEFERRED: will be a recommendation system that doesn't depend on the Spotify API; restock is PHASE 2]
Options for adding new songs could include:
Based on specific song
Based on artist
Based on their taste
Based on genre (might include user taste)
From rotation history
Songs they listen to frequently that aren’t in the rotation
Curated PlayLS pick (daily/weekly song picked by app for users to expand their taste)  [manually curated, PHASE 2]
